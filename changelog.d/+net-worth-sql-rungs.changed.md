Net worth is now three SQL-backed reports — `reports net-worth` (day-grain home total), `reports net-worth-currencies` (currency x day), and `reports net-worth-accounts` (account x day) — replacing the old service-backed `networth`/`networth-history` pair and their `core:networth`/`core:networth_history` ids; use `core:net_worth` / `reports net-worth` with a date range and `--interval daily|weekly|monthly` for a time series instead. `reports.net_worth` changes meaning: it is now the home-currency total rather than the per-currency relation, which moves to `reports.net_worth_currencies` with its columns reordered. `--as-of` and `--account` are gone from every net-worth command. Archived accounts now drop out of the three net-worth reports only after their recorded archive date, not retroactively, and a profile with no home currency gets no net-worth total at all — it fails closed, the same way an unpriced currency already does. A rate recorded with `fx set` or gathered by `refresh` reaches every report immediately, with no separate rebuild step, and `moneybin doctor` gained two gauges: `moneybin_fx_rate_spine_rows` and `moneybin_net_worth_unpriced_dates`.
