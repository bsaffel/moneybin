Net worth is now three SQL-backed reports — `reports net-worth` (day-grain home-currency total), `reports net-worth-currencies` (currency x day), and `reports net-worth-accounts` (account x day) — replacing the `networth` / `networth-history` pair and their `core:networth` / `core:networth_history` ids. Use `core:net_worth` with a date range and `--interval daily|weekly|monthly` for a time series; `--as-of` and `--account` are gone from every net-worth command.
