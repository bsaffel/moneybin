Net worth is now three SQL-backed views and reports instead of one service-backed pair: `reports net-worth` (day-grain home total), `reports net-worth-currencies` (currency x day), and `reports net-worth-accounts` (account x day), each optionally ranged by `--from-date`/`--to-date`. `reports.net_worth` changes meaning — it is now the day-grain home-currency total rather than the per-currency relation, which moves to `reports.net_worth_currencies` with its columns reordered to carry the new rate and freshness data (see `.claude/rules/column-ordering.md`). `core:networth` is now `core:net_worth`; `core:networth_history` and `moneybin reports networth-history` are retired — use `core:net_worth` / `reports net-worth` with a date range and `--interval daily|weekly|monthly` instead. `--as-of` and `--account` are gone from every net-worth command; archived accounts now drop out of the three net-worth reports only after their recorded archive date, not retroactively; and a rate recorded with `moneybin fx set` or gathered by `moneybin refresh` reaches every report immediately, with no separate rebuild step. A profile with no home currency set now gets no net-worth total at all — it fails closed, the same way an unpriced currency already does, while the per-currency and per-account rows stay available. `moneybin doctor` gained two gauges: `moneybin_fx_rate_spine_rows` and `moneybin_net_worth_unpriced_dates`.
