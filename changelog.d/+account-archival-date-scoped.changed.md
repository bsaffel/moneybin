Archiving an account no longer forces `include_in_net_worth` off in the same write: the two are now independent fields, and archiving stamps a new `app.account_settings.archived_at` date instead (cleared on unarchive), which is what will let a net-worth report exclude the account only for dates after it rather than rewriting its whole history. Migration V060 backfills that date for already-archived accounts from the append-only audit history, and the `accounts_set` response drops `cascaded_include_in_net_worth`, which the retired cascade was the only thing that ever set.
