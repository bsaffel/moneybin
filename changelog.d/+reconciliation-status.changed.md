Holdings in an investment account whose history arrives from both manual entry and sync now publish their market value, unrealized gain and pricing instead of reading blank (a position whose share count contradicts the broker snapshot still reads `withheld`), and count toward the portfolio total with a warning naming how many unreconciled accounts it includes. This pre-v1 breaking change removes the `source_overlap` value from `core.dim_holdings.valuation_status` and its `price_resolution_status_total` label; the account-level state moves to `reconciliation_status`.
