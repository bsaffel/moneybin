A PDF that needs AI help is no longer handed to your AI app on first contact: MoneyBin first returns a request with no statement content, and the text is released only after you consent (an MCP prompt, an approval relayed in chat, or `moneybin import preview --allow-ai-disclosure`), with the account numbers, routing numbers and SSNs it identified redacted; an approval relayed by your agent covers one request only, while consent you give MoneyBin directly (its MCP prompt, or `moneybin privacy grant smart-import-parsing --recipient <app>` in your own terminal) can be remembered for an identified app and revoked with `moneybin privacy revoke smart-import-parsing --recipient <app>`.
