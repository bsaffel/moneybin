`core.fct_transactions.is_categorizable` marks the transactions categorization works on: not a confirmed transfer leg, on an account that resolved and is not archived. `sql_query` callers find what still needs a category with `WHERE is_categorizable AND category IS NULL`; the categorization queue, `categorize stats`, and the doctor's coverage check all read this column. Bundle exports of `transactions` carry the column too. Archiving or restoring an account, an undo included, now advances `updated_at` on its transactions and on `core.dim_accounts`, through a new `app.account_settings.archived_changed_at`.
